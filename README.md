# 三千世界 · Three Thousand Worlds

让故事生长出更多世界。 / Let stories grow into more worlds.

[中文介绍](README.zh-CN.md) · [Whitepaper / 白皮书](WHITEPAPER.md) · [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) · [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Rights](RIGHTS.md)

## An open creative community, starting small

Three Thousand Worlds is an early-stage initiative for independent IP rights holders and creators worldwide. It explores collaboration across multiple story worlds while keeping each IP's rights and commercial authorization decisions with its rights holders.

**Version: v0.1 · 2026-10-31 · Public discussion draft**

The first pilot is **Shenjin (《申金》)**. “Parallel Universes” describes alternative story branches and interpretations within an IP; it is not a separate platform.

## What exists today

- A public proposal, roadmap, contribution guide and pilot introduction.
- This repository as a versioned documentation and feedback entry point.

We do not yet operate a multi-IP service, automated revenue attribution, a public fund, voting system or commercial settlement service. Proposed mechanisms are not contractual promises.

## Principles

- Joining does not transfer IP ownership to the platform.
- Noncommercial fan creation is intended to be permitted within each IP's published license.
- Creators' original contributions should not automatically become platform or original-IP property.
- Canon status, community participation, fund governance and commercial authorization are separate.
- AI may assist production and operations; people remain accountable for rights and financial decisions.

**Public does not mean unrestricted reuse.** No blanket open-source or creative-content license is granted by this release. IP-specific permissions and software licenses will be stated separately; see [Rights](RIGHTS.md).

## Participate

Read the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md), then open an [issue](https://github.com/yud9804-art/three-thousand-worlds/issues) for a question, proposal, translation offer or pilot expression of interest. Participation does not promise selection, payment or official canon status.

The full v0.1 proposal is currently in Chinese. This English introduction is a summary. Translations are welcome and will be reviewed before being treated as reference versions.
